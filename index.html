scenarioflow

scenarioflow assembles the time-dependent inputs to numerical models from sources that differ in provenance, certainty, duration and resolution: historical archives, real-time or provisional data, forecasts, climatology and planning assumptions.

Each conceptual input (a river flow, a gate operation, …) is built from ordered components, each read and transformed independently, and then spliced together in time at explicit effective dates.
